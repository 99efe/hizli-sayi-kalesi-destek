RoutineCove — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

We collect no data. RoutineCove stores your routines and run history only as a local file on your device. The app has no account system, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple Account; we never see your payment details. Notification permission, if granted, is used only to notify you locally when a routine step ends — scheduling happens entirely on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all app data. Nothing is retained anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.
