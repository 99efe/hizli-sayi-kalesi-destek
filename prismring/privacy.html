Prism Ring: Glass Shooter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

We collect no data. Prism Ring stores your progress and records only as local data on your device. The app has no account system, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center service under Apple's own privacy terms; we never receive or store them ourselves. Playing without Game Center keeps everything on your device.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple Account; we never see your payment details.

Deleting the app deletes all app data. Nothing is retained anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.
