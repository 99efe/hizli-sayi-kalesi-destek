English

Hızlı Sayı Kalesi collects no personal data. The app has no accounts, shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics or tracking.

  • Game progress and hint balance are stored only on your device and never sent to us.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • Leaderboards work through optional Game Center under Apple's own privacy terms. If you don't use Game Center, no scores are shared.
