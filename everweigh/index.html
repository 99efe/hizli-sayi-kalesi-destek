Frequently asked

How is my budget calculated? Open Settings → “How this is calculated”. BMR uses Mifflin–St Jeor, expenditure is estimated from your own weight trend and logged intake.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Export it any time from Settings.

How do I cancel Premium? In your Apple Account settings under Subscriptions.
