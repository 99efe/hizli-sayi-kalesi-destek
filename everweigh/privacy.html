Everweigh — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Your data stays on your device. Everweigh stores your profile, goals, meals, water and weight entries only on your device. There is no account system, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Food search. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the app sends only your search text or the barcode number to two public databases: USDA FoodData Central (United States Department of Agriculture) and Open Food Facts. No profile, weight or identity information is sent with those requests. Manual entry works without any network access. Barcode data is provided by Open Food Facts under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Subscriptions. Everweigh Premium is processed by Apple through your Apple Account; we never see your payment details.

Health disclaimer. Everweigh is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Nothing in the app is a diagnosis or treatment.

Deleting your data. You can export your data from Settings at any time. Deleting the app deletes all app data. Nothing is retained anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.
